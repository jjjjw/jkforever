Vows
Intro
Lola:

A few years ago, I started what might be the most successful Moby Dick bookclub of all time, with one friend in North Carolina, JJ in Oakland, and Kristen in Boston. For 3 months we met online every single week for at least an hour to talk about the book. No one ever skipped a meeting. Later that year, Kristen moved back to the Bay Area and asked JJ to hang out. The rest is history.

People sometimes assume I intentionally set them up, I honestly just wanted to read Moby Dick. Kristen and JJ found each other without anyone's help because they were meant to. You were once Loose Fish, & now you are Fast Fish. So without further ado...

We are gathered here in the presence of witnesses for the purpose of uniting in matrimony Kristen and JJ.

The contract of marriage is most solemn and is not to be entered into lightly, but thoughtfully and seriously with a deep realization of its obligations and responsibilities.

JJ and Kristen, no other words of mine or any other person truly marry each of you to the other. That is done when you exchange your promises and commit yourselves to this marriage and each other.

By entering into this marriage, you are pledging yourselves to a lifetime in which each will enrich the life of the other. You will be partners standing together to cushion the difficulties of life. Rejoice in your partner's graces. Nurture your marriage carefully and watch it grow.

If anyone has cause to object to the forming of this union, speak now or forever hold your peace.

JJ and Kristen, I invite you to express your vows to one another. Please face each other and join hands.

Vows
JJ:

Kristen,

It's wonderful to be with you here today. I'm excited to take this leap together.

When I was on the way to meet you for our first date, I was very excited to talk to you. I thought I had something important to say to you. This was delusional. I don't remember what we talked on our first date. We were both so nervous. I actually thought it had gone badly, and that I wouldn't see you again. So I was surprised and elated when you reached out only a couple days later asking if I would join you for a laptop music show. From then on, I was constantly thinking about when I would see you next. The frenzied excitement of falling in love was so fun, but I didn't know in those first few months just how good this would be. I'm stunned all the time, by your beauty and by the life I have with you.

The last 2.5 years together have been so full of happiness for me. It rapidly became uncanny how many similar interests we have. Some things I used to do alone—niche electronic music shows, long hikes, reading 19th century novels—now you're there with me. I can share so much with you, and I feel like I can talk to you about anything. We can even talk about our jobs writing software. And because we're both sick in a similar way, we actually do talk about software. I feel incredibly lucky to have found this companionship with you.

Today, it feels easy to promise to always love you, and to always be honest and true. Marrying you is one of the easiest choices of my life. In time, it might not always be so easy. But I know that if I can pay attention and see you, there will always be a beautiful, caring, thoughtful, and funny person that I am forever grateful for. I promise not to lose sight of you.

I promise to keep you by my side through good times and bad, for richer or poorer, in sickness and in health. I vow to stay true to you, honor you, and love you for the rest of my days, until death do us part.

Kristen:

JJ,

At a perhaps startlingly early moment in our relationship, you asked what I thought about marriage – because of Swann and Odette, no other reason. I returned the question. Your answer was extremely simple. You said, “Vows are metal.”

I've heard that when entering into a marriage, you should ask yourself: do you believe you are going to enjoy talking with this person into your old age? Everything else in marriage is transitory; most of the time you are together will be devoted to conversation.

I thought you were interesting the moment I met you. As we got to know each other better, we talked about all sorts of things: ourselves, our days, and often books. It felt startling at first, all the things we could talk about. There is a specific kind of luck in finding a person whose mind operates at the same frequency as your own.

I have been so happy with you. There is a lot of art about falling in love – a wild, frenetic quality that is universal and impossible to capture. Sometimes you mention wishing we'd met sooner, but I feel so happy we met when we did. We met at a point in our lives where we are sure of ourselves and sure of each other. We each have the self-possession to take this leap together.

There is so much I love about you – I love your romanticism, your humor, your commitment to do right by those in your life. To me, you are the fixed point, a constant. There is a sense of infinite possibility with you.

I am excited for our marriage because I am excited for the shared project of it. I am excited to build our life together. You are my favorite collaborator, my bright star. I am excited to see you every day, but above all else, I am excited to keep talking to you. I promise not to lose sight of you.

I promise to keep you by my side through good times and bad, for richer or poorer, in sickness and in health. I vow to stay true to you, honor you, and love you for the rest of my days, until death do us part.
